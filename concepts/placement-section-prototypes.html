I rebuilt how Next fills in product details, so AI does the typing and people check it.

Every product on next.co.uk needs details like age group, material and occasion before it can go live. Staff used to pick each one from dropdowns. I owned the rebuild that has Next's in-house AI, Shield, suggest them instead.

What I built

  • The review screen editors use, where AI suggestions they're confident in (90%+) approve themselves.
  • A feedback loop that records whether each suggestion was accepted, changed or rejected, so the AI team can improve the model.
  • A rules engine so the business decides which fields AI may fill and which always need a person.

Highlighted by CEO Simon Wolfson in Next's half-year results.

Before

An editor fills every field by hand, product after product.

After

AI fills the fields. Confident answers go straight through; editors only check the rest. Every correction feeds back to the model.
