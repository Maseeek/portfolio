Theme icon lab

Five sun ⇄ moon morphs built for the portfolio's light/dark toggle (Nov 2026). Click a card, or its real-size button, to switch that card's theme.

On the real page the background uses the site's 250ms View Transition crossfade, and the icon animates live above it on its own transition layer (view-transition-name on the button). This file is self-contained, so it keeps working after the prototype harness is deleted.
